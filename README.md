# Lily - she wants to play

![Lily](preview.jpg)

On the Steam Workshop: [Lily](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3837310472)

A little girl is somewhere on this map. You may hear her before you see her: humming, skipping, crying in a corner. Whoever finds her, or whoever she finds, is hers to play with. Works on any map, alone or with friends.

**Jump scare warning:** this mod has sudden jump scares with loud screams and a face filling the screen, plus flickering lights. You can turn the kills off in the mod's options, but not the scares.

## How she haunts
- She **plays, cries or roams** where nobody is looking. Follow the sound and you will find her. Then she looks at you, and she is gone.
- For the next few minutes she plays with whoever found her. She **whispers in one ear** and nobody else hears it. She stands at the far end of the street, or **right behind you**, and is gone when you look. Sometimes only her victim can see her.
- Before she comes, **lights flicker and go out**, flashlights fail and **loose things lift off the ground**. Sometimes nothing follows.
- Nothing happens on a schedule. Long silences, two scares in a row, false alarms: you can't tell when.

## When she is done playing
- Her laughter turns into a **cackle** that everyone hears, the lights go out, and she comes for you: running, skipping, floating, or **scuttling on all fours, belly up**.
- She walks **through walls** and breaks nothing.
- She finds you by **sight and sound**. Running, shooting and talking (with Proximity Comms) give you away. Get out of her sight and stay quiet, and she loses you.
- **Bullets don't hurt her.** A hit only sends her somewhere else around you, never right next to you: keep shooting and she can't reach you. For a while.
- If she catches you, you die. Then she is calm again, and it all starts over.

## Settings
**Options** in the Mod Manager:
- How often she acts: rarely, sometimes, often.
- She hunts, or she only scares.
- Her whispers as faint text: shown or hidden.

## Multiplayer
The host turns it on under Global mods; the host's settings count. Whispers and jump scares go to one player only.

## For mod and map makers
A script on the host can make her act through one registry string: **lily.cmd** = "scene", "haunt", "hunt" or "sleep".
