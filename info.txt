description = A little girl is somewhere on this map. You may hear her first: humming, skipping, crying in a corner. Whoever finds her - or whoever she finds - is hers to play with. She whispers in one ear only, stands at the end of the corridor, stands behind you, and is gone when you look. Before she comes, lights flicker and die and loose things lift off the floor. Sometimes nothing follows. When she is done playing her laughter turns into a cackle, and then she comes for you: running, skipping, scuttling on all fours, through walls. Bullets do not hurt her, they only send her somewhere else around you. Works on any map, single player and multiplayer. Options: how often she acts, whether she kills, whisper text.
